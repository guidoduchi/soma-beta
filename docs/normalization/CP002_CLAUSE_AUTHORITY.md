# SOMA Beta Normalization CP-002 — Clause Authority Matrix

Status: **Accepted**  
Scope: all **275** stable clauses owned by `BETA-REQ-0028`–`BETA-REQ-0052`.  
Purpose: explicit clause-level evidence for the CP-002 reverse-authority audit.

## Reading the matrix

- **Owner** is the normalized requirement under which the clause is stored.
- **Supporting authority** is recorded only when the clause relies on additional approved product authority or an explicit project-owner clarification.
- `—` means the owning requirement fully establishes the clause.
- Supporting authority does **not** transfer ownership and does not create new behavior.
- Technical design items identify accepted design-resolution boundaries; they do not add product behavior.
- The explicit `BETA-REQ-0037` backup-set amendment records the project owner's normalization-time clarification requiring MDA-style digest/manifest and PKCS#7/CMS-style detached companion artifacts, or governed equivalents.
- This matrix is evidence only; normative rule text remains in `CP002_CLAUSES.md`.
