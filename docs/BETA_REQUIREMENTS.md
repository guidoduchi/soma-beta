| `BETA-REQ-0013` | A WFM business identifier shall consist of `TK` followed by exactly fourteen decimal digits. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-004` | Data Model; identifier contract; WFM import contract; manual WFM registration workflow | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0014` | Every Spare Request shall originate locally in SOMA Beta and receive an immutable, non-reusable, chronologically sortable, collision-safe temporary tracking identifier at creation. Assignment of the permanent identifier shall not replace the entity's internal identity or erase its temporary tracking identifier. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-005`; confirmed Spare Request identity lifecycle | Inventory Data Model; identifier contract; Spare Request lifecycle; communication reconciliation; UI | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0015` | A Huawei RMA identifier shall consist of `C` followed by exactly ten decimal digits and shall belong to exactly one officially identified Spare Request. While awaiting receipt, an RMA may have no received Spare Part Unit; once fulfilled, it shall identify exactly one received unit, and a received unit shall belong to at most one RMA. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-006`; confirmed RMA lifecycle | Inventory Data Model; identifier contract; receiving workflow; reconciliation rules | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0016` | A manually registered Spare Part Unit shall receive an immutable, non-reusable local identifier in the form `LSU-########`, allocated incrementally beginning with `LSU-00000001`. If an RMA is supplied, its parent Spare Request shall be derived from the RMA and inconsistent combinations shall be rejected. | `BETA-REQ-0017` | SOMA Beta shall persist scheduled instants in canonical UTC with second-level precision and shall display them in the operator-configured local time zone. Imported timestamps shall preserve their source meaning and provenance. Task and Objective boundaries may occur at any valid minute and shall not be silently rounded or forced onto half-hour boundaries. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-007`; confirmed scheduling precision decision | Data Model; scheduling contract; import normalization; Settings; Objective UI; overlap algorithm | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0018` | SOMA Beta's persistence boundary shall reject missing or structurally invalid required values, broken relationships, duplicate constrained identities, invalid enumerated states, and malformed structured data. Referential, uniqueness, nullability, and structurally enforceable constraints shall be protected by the database; domain-dependent rules shall be validated transactionally before commit. UI, import, and background workflows shall not bypass these invariants. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-008`; confirmed clean-schema decision | Data Model; persistence LLD; service transaction contract; import pipeline; validation tests | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0019` | SOMA Beta 1.0 shall preserve imported, referenced, or operationally processed history through explicit lifecycle states such as archived, cancelled, rejected, superseded, or rolled back, together with audit evidence. Hard deletion shall be permitted only for manually created records that were never import-populated, never participated in an executed Maintenance Window, and have no protected dependent history, or where an explicit domain correction rule authorizes deletion. Any dependent deletion shall be previewed, confirmed, transactional, and audited. General purge policies are deferred to Beta 1.1. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-009`; confirmed deletion and purge decisions | Product Contract; retention contract; audit contract; domain lifecycle specifications; destructive-action UI | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0020` | Mutable logistics master data shall not rewrite historical delivery or pickup evidence. Logistics operations shall preserve immutable Dispatch Location snapshots where needed to represent the names, addresses, and related facts that were effective when the operation occurred. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-010` | Inventory Data Model; Dispatch Location contract; Spare Request lifecycle; Fault Tag workflow; audit/history views | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0021` | SOMA Beta shall manage Customer Organizations and Contacts. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-001` | Settings workspace; reference Data Model; Contact management use cases; ticket workbenches | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0022` | Each SOMA Beta installation shall maintain exactly one authenticating Local User Profile acting as local administrator. Operational people—including requesters, handlers, owners, executors, and customer contacts—shall be reusable Contact records rather than login profiles, and one person may fulfill multiple operational roles. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-002`; confirmed single-user and registered-person decisions | Product Contract; Settings workspace; identity model; Contact model; authentication LLD; operational selectors | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0023` | Dispatch Locations shall represent named, addressed physical logistics locations reusable across Spare Request and Fault Tag workflows. A Dispatch Location shall not imply ownership by a Customer Organization and shall remain distinct from a Datacenter Site. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-003`; confirmed Dispatch Location terminology and semantics | Inventory Data Model; Settings workspace; Spare Request workflow; Fault Tag workflow; Glossary | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0024` | A Site shall represent a Datacenter infrastructure location and shall remain a distinct entity from a Dispatch Location. Registering a Site shall automatically create and link one dedicated Dispatch Location. A Dispatch Location may exist without a Site but shall link to at most one Site; a Site shall link to exactly one Dispatch Location. When linked, the Dispatch Location shall inherit the Site's effective address. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-004`; confirmed Site and Dispatch Location decisions | Infrastructure Data Model; Settings workspace; Site creation workflow; Dispatch Location workflow; address rules | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0025` | Reference records with operational history shall support archival. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-005` | Reference Data Model; Settings workspace; historical views; selectors; retention contract | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0026` | A Dispatch Location shall not be owned by, directly assigned to, or marked preferred for a Customer Organization. Any Customer Organization context visible for a Dispatch Location shall be derived from its optional linked Site and shall not redefine the Dispatch Location's physical identity or prevent its use in an otherwise valid logistics operation. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-006`; confirmed customer-neutral Dispatch Location decision | Reference Data Model; Dispatch Location contract; Settings workspace; logistics selectors; migration/reuse review | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0027` | Every Site shall represent one physical Datacenter and belong to exactly one Customer Organization. Site names, aliases, and city codes may repeat across Customer Organizations and shall not constitute identity. Every Cloud Deployment shall belong to exactly one Site and reference a reusable Cloud Type. A regularized Network Element shall derive its Customer Organization through its Site; any assigned Cloud Deployment shall belong to that same Site. Provisional or external Device references may remain without Site or Cloud placement until regularized. When creating a Site, possible duplicate physical locations identified through normalized address comparison shall be presented for operator review and shall not be merged automatically. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-007`; confirmed physical and logical Infrastructure hierarchy | Infrastructure Data Model; Site and Cloud contracts; Device regularization contract; Settings; Ticket and Inventory relationships | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0028` | Every Contact shall use an opaque internal SOMA identifier as its relational identity. Names shall be descriptive and may collide. A normalized email address shall be matching data within the applicable organizational or unbound scope, not a global identifier or universal uniqueness constraint. When available evidence matches multiple Contacts, SOMA shall require operator review and shall not merge or select a Contact automatically. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-008`; confirmed generalized Contact model | Contact Data Model; Settings workspace; import reconciliation; operational selectors; reviewed-merge UI | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0029` | A Contact's identity shall remain independent of Customer Organization affiliation. A Contact may have no current Customer Organization or at most one current affiliation. Changing that affiliation shall preserve the same Contact, close the previous affiliation as historical evidence, create the new affiliation, and record the change in the audit log. Equal names, including equal names across different organizations, shall never establish shared identity or trigger an affiliation change; such a change requires explicit operator identification of the existing Contact using stronger evidence. Operational records shall preserve the Contact and Customer Organization context effective when the relationship was recorded. If a selected Contact is currently affiliated with a different Customer Organization than a Service Request, SOMA shall warn the operator and require reviewed confirmation or correction; it shall not silently reassign or duplicate the Contact or reject an imported Service Request solely because of the mismatch. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-009`; confirmed Contact-affiliation and duplicate-name decisions | Contact Data Model; affiliation history; SR workbench; import reconciliation; audit contract | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0030` | Advanced Search reference candidate matching shall use deterministic exact keys produced through Unicode NFKC compatibility normalization, collapsing surrounding and internal Unicode whitespace, and Unicode case folding. Matching shall not strip accents or punctuation and shall not use fuzzy similarity. If multiple active records within the applicable scope produce the same key, the result shall be ambiguous and SOMA shall require operator review rather than guess. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-010` | Import Contract; reconciliation service; Contact matching; Customer Organization matching; test vectors | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0031` | A Contact may be created without a Customer Organization or with one active Customer Organization affiliation. Affiliation, reassignment, and removal shall follow the reviewed historical rules in `BETA-REQ-0029`. Archived Contacts and other archived master records shall remain visible in historical evidence but shall not receive new active operational relationships. Archival shall be blocked when it would strand an active dependent record; the dependency must first be completed, cancelled, reassigned, or otherwise resolved through an authorized workflow. Reactivation shall be explicit and audited. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-011`; confirmed optional Contact affiliation | Contact lifecycle; reference-data lifecycle; Settings workspace; selectors; dependency validation; audit contract | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0032` | A Spare Request's requester shall reference a reusable Contact acting in the Requester role. An archived Contact shall not be selectable for new Spare Requests. A Contact shall not be archived while serving as requester for any active, nonterminal Spare Request. Terminal Spare Request history shall retain the Contact reference and the requester evidence effective when the request was created. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-012`; confirmed reusable Contact-role model | Spare Request Data Model; Contact role model; requester selector; archival validation; historical views | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0033` | A Site's Customer Organization ownership shall be stable and shall not be transferable through ordinary editing. An explicitly reasoned and audited correction may change an erroneous initial Customer Organization only before the Site, any Cloud Deployment, or any placed Network Element has acquired operational history. Site archival shall be blocked while it would strand active Cloud Deployments, Rooms, Racks, Network Elements, Tasks, Objectives, Spare Needs, Spare Requests, or other unfinished operational relationships. Archival shall preserve the Site's dedicated Dispatch Location and all historical evidence unless a separately reviewed lifecycle action authorizes another result. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-013`; confirmed Site ownership and expanded dependency model | Infrastructure lifecycle; Settings workspace; ownership correction workflow; dependency validation; Inventory integration; audit contract | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0034` | Dispatch Locations shall not maintain Customer Organization availability or preference relationships. An archived Dispatch Location shall remain visible in historical evidence but shall not be selectable for new Spare Request, dispatch, pickup, or Fault Tag operations. A Dispatch Location shall not be archived while linked to an active Site or referenced by an unfinished logistics operation. A Site-dedicated Dispatch Location shall not be archived independently of a reviewed Site lifecycle action. Reactivation shall be explicit and audited. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-014`; confirmed customer-neutral Dispatch Location lifecycle | Dispatch Location lifecycle; Inventory workflows; Settings workspace; dependency validation; audit/history views | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0035` | The singleton Local User Profile shall be configured once. Its password shall be used only to authenticate the local administrator and shall be stored only as a salted, memory-hard verifier. The username, password, and other profile fields shall not constitute or derive encryption keys. Optional automatic login shall use Windows-protected authentication material and shall not store the password. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-015`; confirmed authentication boundary | Identity contract; Settings; authentication workflow; Security LLD | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0036` | SOMA Beta shall encrypt protected local data using authenticated encryption and a cryptographically random data-encryption key of at least 256 bits, generated independently of the local administrator's credentials. The key shall be protected using an approved Windows-bound secure-storage mechanism. Changing or resetting the application password shall not re-encrypt, invalidate, or endanger operational data. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-015`; confirmed live-data encryption boundary | Persistence contract; Security LLD; key lifecycle; acceptance tests | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0037` | Each exportable backup shall be protected with authenticated encryption using an independent random backup-encryption key. Portable recovery shall use a generated high-entropy recovery secret—recommended as seven randomly selected words or an equivalent secret of at least 128 bits—not a seven-character password. Cryptographic hashes shall verify integrity and shall not substitute for encryption. A restore shall authenticate and verify the backup before modifying live data. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:REF-015`; confirmed portable-backup protection boundary | Backup/recovery contract; Security LLD; export workflow; restore acceptance tests | Confirmed |
