# SOMA Beta Requirements Register

Status: **Working draft — Batch 1 review in progress**  
Identifiers are immutable and intentionally neutral. Domain, release, status, source, and destination are metadata rather than part of the identifier.

| Beta ID | Normative requirement | Release | Source traceability | Destination | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BETA-REQ-0001` | SOMA Beta shall maintain an independent product, repository, schema, migration, and release lineage. Zeus and SOMA Alpha are historical sources only; reuse requires explicit evaluation and does not imply product or data continuity. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:PROD-001` | Product Contract §2 | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0002` | SOMA Beta shall provide its primary operator experience as a localhost web application backed by Python. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:PROD-002` | Product Contract §3; Architecture §§2 and 8 | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0003` | SOMA Beta operational data shall remain locally usable without a separately administered database server. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:PROD-003` | Product Contract §3; Architecture §§4–5 | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0004` | SOMA Beta 1.0 shall support 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11 with Python 3.13 and Python 3.14. Other operating systems are not Beta 1.0 support targets. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:PROD-004` | Architecture §§8–9; Roadmap acceptance matrix | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0005` | All SOMA Beta source code, documentation, brand assets, and release artifacts shall remain private proprietary material authorized only for the internal team. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:PROD-005`; confirmed Beta ownership decision | Product Contract §2; Roadmap licensing boundary | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0006` | The project-owner-owned canonical SOMA brand assets shall be relicensed into Beta as proprietary assets with internal provenance recorded. The private SOMA Alpha repository shall retain its existing Apache-2.0 license, but that license shall not govern or be inherited by SOMA Beta. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:PROD-005`; confirmed exclusive ownership and Alpha licensing decision | Branding Contract; Product Contract §2 | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0007` | SOMA Beta shall never commit operational or customer data, imported workbooks, PST/OST/MSG communication material, credentials or cryptographic keys, runtime state, logs or diagnostics, databases or journals, backups, exports, or generated operational artifacts to source control. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:PROD-006` | Product Contract §§11–14; Import Contract §2; Architecture §5; repository security policy | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0008` | Before SOMA Beta 1.0 acceptance, every catalogued merged Alpha requirement and decision, clearly marked unmerged Alpha proposal, and approved user-facing Zeus capability shall receive an explicit Beta disposition. Every retained or replaced capability shall trace to its normative Beta requirement, target release, business use case, design destination, and implemented UI/UX workflow; deferred and rejected items shall retain an explicit rationale. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:PROD-007`; confirmed Beta traceability decisions | Alpha Traceability Register; Requirements Register; Use-Case Catalogue; Roadmap acceptance gates; UI/UX traceability | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0009` | SOMA Beta shall provide a repeatable local setup process and explicit commands for starting and stopping the application. Windows source deployments shall include direct launchers for those operations. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:PROD-008` | Architecture deployment model; LLD deployment specification; operator setup guide; acceptance tests | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0010` | Every persistent SOMA Beta entity shall have an immutable, opaque internal identifier independent of its externally assigned or locally generated business identifiers. Business identifiers shall be stored as validated domain attributes and shall not serve as relational primary or foreign keys. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-001`; confirmed clean-schema decision | Data Model; identifier contract; database LLD; import reconciliation rules | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0011` | An externally assigned Service Request business identifier shall canonicalize to exactly eight decimal digits. Any `SR` or `TT` label supplied by a source or displayed by the interface shall not form part of that canonical identity. A Service Request created without an external identifier shall receive a non-reusable local identifier in the form `LSR-########`, allocated incrementally beginning with `LSR-00000001`. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-002`; confirmed manual-SR identity decision | Data Model; identifier contract; SR import contract; SR creation workflow | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0012` | A canonical RFC business identifier shall consist of `NC` followed by exactly fourteen decimal digits. An imported RFC identifier containing additional characters or digits beyond that canonical form shall be treated as a source branch artifact: it shall not create or update an RFC record and shall be reported as skipped in the reviewed import result. Other malformed RFC identifiers shall fail validation rather than being classified as branch artifacts. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-003`; confirmed RFC branch-artifact decision | Data Model; identifier contract; RFC import contract; import-review UI | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0013` | A WFM business identifier shall consist of `TK` followed by exactly fourteen decimal digits. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-004` | Data Model; identifier contract; WFM import contract; manual WFM registration workflow | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0014` | Every Spare Request shall originate locally in SOMA Beta and receive an immutable, non-reusable, chronologically sortable, collision-safe temporary tracking identifier at creation. When Huawei assigns the permanent identifier—`SR` followed by exactly seven decimal digits—it shall be attached to the existing Spare Request. Assignment of the permanent identifier shall not replace the entity's internal identity or erase its temporary tracking identifier. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-005`; confirmed Spare Request identity lifecycle | Inventory Data Model; identifier contract; Spare Request lifecycle; communication reconciliation; UI | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0015` | A Huawei RMA identifier shall consist of `C` followed by exactly ten decimal digits and shall belong to exactly one officially identified Spare Request. While awaiting receipt, an RMA may have no received Spare Part Unit; once fulfilled, it shall identify exactly one received unit, and a received unit shall belong to at most one RMA. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-006`; confirmed RMA lifecycle | Inventory Data Model; identifier contract; receiving workflow; reconciliation rules | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0016` | A manually registered Spare Part Unit shall receive an immutable, non-reusable local identifier in the form `LSU-########`, allocated incrementally beginning with `LSU-00000001`. Its official Spare Request and RMA references shall be optional, allowing old or otherwise untrackable stock to remain valid. If an RMA is supplied, its parent Spare Request shall be derived from the RMA and inconsistent combinations shall be rejected. The LSU identifier shall remain permanently available if official identifiers or a serial number are attached later. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-006`; confirmed local Spare Unit identity | Inventory Data Model; identifier contract; manual stock registration; reconciliation rules | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0017` | SOMA Beta shall persist scheduled instants in canonical UTC with second-level precision and shall display them in the operator-configured local time zone. Imported timestamps shall preserve their source meaning and provenance. Task and Objective boundaries may occur at any valid minute and shall not be silently rounded or forced onto half-hour boundaries. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-007`; confirmed scheduling precision decision | Data Model; scheduling contract; import normalization; Settings; Objective UI; overlap algorithm | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0018` | SOMA Beta's persistence boundary shall reject missing or structurally invalid required values, broken relationships, duplicate constrained identities, invalid enumerated states, and malformed structured data. Referential, uniqueness, nullability, and structurally enforceable constraints shall be protected by the database; domain-dependent rules shall be validated transactionally before commit. UI, import, and background workflows shall not bypass these invariants. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-008`; confirmed clean-schema decision | Data Model; persistence LLD; service transaction contract; import pipeline; validation tests | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0019` | SOMA Beta 1.0 shall preserve imported, referenced, or operationally processed history through explicit lifecycle states such as archived, cancelled, rejected, superseded, or rolled back, together with audit evidence. Hard deletion shall be permitted only for manually created records that were never import-populated, never participated in an executed Maintenance Window, and have no protected dependent history, or where an explicit domain correction rule authorizes deletion. Any dependent deletion shall be previewed, confirmed, transactional, and audited. General purge policies are deferred to Beta 1.1. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-009`; confirmed deletion and purge decisions | Product Contract; retention contract; audit contract; domain lifecycle specifications; destructive-action UI | Confirmed |
| `BETA-REQ-0020` | Mutable logistics master data shall not rewrite historical delivery or pickup evidence. Logistics operations shall preserve immutable Dispatch Location snapshots where needed to represent the names, addresses, and related facts that were effective when the operation occurred. | 1.0.0 | `ALPHA:DATA-010` | Inventory Data Model; Dispatch Location contract; Spare Request lifecycle; Fault Tag workflow; audit/history views | Confirmed |
