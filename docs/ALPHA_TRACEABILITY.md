# SOMA Beta Alpha Traceability Register

Status: **Working draft — Batch 1 review in progress**  
Scope: one row per SOMA Alpha requirement or decision ID. Proposal revisions of an existing ID are recorded in the same row rather than creating duplicate identities.

| Alpha ID | Alpha authority | Alpha statement | Beta disposition | Beta requirement | Beta release | Normative destination | Gap | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ALPHA:PROD-001` | Merged `main` | SOMA shall be a clean Service Operations Management Application, not a rename/history continuation of Zeus. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0001` | 1.0.0 | Product Contract §2 | — | Beta preserves the clean-lineage intent but broadens the historical boundary to both Zeus and SOMA Alpha and requires explicit evaluation of any reuse. |
| `ALPHA:PROD-002` | Merged `main` | The intended UX shall be a localhost web application backed by Python. | Retain | `BETA-REQ-0002` | 1.0.0 | Product Contract §3; Architecture §§2 and 8 | — | The local Python-backed web application remains the accepted product direction. Responsive behavior, offline operation, frontend technology, and loopback security are governed separately. |
| `ALPHA:PROD-003` | Merged `main` | Operational data shall remain locally usable without a separately administered database server. | Retain | `BETA-REQ-0003` | 1.0.0 | Product Contract §3; Architecture §§4–5 | — | Beta remains locally usable and application-managed; authentication and encryption do not introduce a separately administered database service. |
| `ALPHA:PROD-004` | Merged `main` | Supported product targets are 64-bit Windows 10/11 with Python 3.13 or 3.14. Other OSes are not support targets. | Retain | `BETA-REQ-0004` | 1.0.0 | Architecture §§8–9; Roadmap acceptance matrix | Narrows `O-006` | Beta retains the proven Alpha support targets. Acceptance must cover all four Windows/Python combinations; browser, Windows-edition/build, and packaging details remain assigned to LLD. |
| `ALPHA:PROD-005` | Merged `main` | Source shall remain distributable under Apache License 2.0 with applicable attribution retained. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0005`, `BETA-REQ-0006` | 1.0.0 | Product Contract §2; Branding Contract; Roadmap licensing boundary | — | Beta is wholly private and proprietary. The project owner confirms exclusive rights in the canonical brand assets and relicenses them separately into Beta. The Alpha repository is also private, but retains its existing Apache-2.0 license; repository visibility does not transfer that license to Beta. |
| `ALPHA:PROD-006` | Merged `main` | Runtime/customer/mail/credential/log/database/backup/export data shall not be committed to source control. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0007` | 1.0.0 | Product Contract §§11–14; Import Contract §2; Architecture §5; repository security policy | — | Beta retains the source-control exclusion and expands it to cover every operational input, communication artifact, secret, persistence artifact, backup, export, and generated operational artifact handled by the broader Beta scope. |
| `ALPHA:PROD-007` | Merged `main` | SOMA 1 stable shall meet or deliberately replace every approved user-facing Zeus capability through explicit parity disposition. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0008` | 1.0.0 | Alpha Traceability Register; Requirements Register; Use-Case Catalogue; Roadmap acceptance gates; UI/UX traceability | — | Beta broadens explicit parity beyond Zeus to merged Alpha requirements and decisions and clearly marked unmerged Alpha proposals, while requiring downstream traceability through use cases, design, implementation, and UI/UX. |
| `ALPHA:PROD-008` | Merged `main` | SOMA shall provide repeatable local setup plus explicit run/stop commands; Windows source deployments include direct launchers. | Retain | `BETA-REQ-0009` | 1.0.0 | Architecture deployment model; LLD deployment specification; operator setup guide; acceptance tests | Assigned LLD detail | The repeatable local setup and direct Windows start/stop launcher requirement remains valid for Beta's offline local deployment model. Launcher format, process supervision, clean shutdown, and failure reporting belong in the LLD. |
| `ALPHA:DATA-001` | Merged `main` | Internal SOMA IDs are independent strings; external business identifiers are not relational primary keys. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0010` | 1.0.0 | Data Model; identifier contract; database LLD; import reconciliation rules | Assigned LLD detail | Beta retains independent internal identity but avoids prematurely requiring one storage representation. Opaque internal IDs protect relationships when imported or locally entered business identifiers are corrected. The identifier format and generation algorithm belong in the LLD. |
| `ALPHA:DATA-002` | Merged `main` | Service Request identity is exactly eight digits. `SR`/`TT` are display contexts only. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0011` | 1.0.0 | Data Model; identifier contract; SR import contract; SR creation workflow | Assigned LLD detail | Beta retains the canonical eight-digit external SR identity and adds non-reusable incremental `LSR-########` identities for locally created SRs that lack an external identifier. Sequence allocation belongs in the LLD. |
| `ALPHA:DATA-003` | Merged `main` | RFC identity is `NC` plus fourteen digits. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0012` | 1.0.0 | Data Model; identifier contract; RFC import contract; import-review UI | — | Beta retains the canonical RFC format and adds the confirmed rule that overlength source identifiers represent branch artifacts, do not create or update RFC records, and appear as skipped in the reviewed import result. |
| `ALPHA:DATA-004` | Merged `main` | WFM identity is `TK` plus fourteen digits. | Retain | `BETA-REQ-0013` | 1.0.0 | Data Model; identifier contract; WFM import contract; manual WFM registration workflow | — | The canonical WFM identity remains unchanged. Local Tasks are a distinct Task category and cannot carry or imitate the WFM identifier format. |
| `ALPHA:DATA-005` | Merged `main` | Permanent Spare Request identity is `SR` plus seven digits; pre-assignment tracking ID is twelve digits. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0014` | 1.0.0 | Inventory Data Model; identifier contract; Spare Request lifecycle; communication reconciliation; UI | Assigned LLD detail | Every Beta Spare Request originates locally, receives an immutable temporary tracking identifier, and later acquires the permanent Huawei `SR#######` identifier on the same entity. Beta preserves both references but replaces the unexplained fixed twelve-digit constraint with an explicit collision-safe, chronologically sortable identifier whose concrete format belongs in the LLD. |
| `ALPHA:DATA-006` | Merged `main` | Requested physical spare identity is `C` plus ten digits. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0015`, `BETA-REQ-0016` | 1.0.0 | Inventory Data Model; identifier contract; receiving workflow; manual stock registration; reconciliation rules | Assigned LLD detail | Beta distinguishes the Huawei RMA/order position from the received physical unit and adds `LSU-########` identity for manually registered units whose official Spare Request, RMA, or serial provenance may be absent. Sequence allocation and display/search details belong in the LLD. |
| `ALPHA:DATA-007` | Merged `main` | Canonical scheduled timestamps use UTC `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`; MW boundaries fall on `:00`/`:30`. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0017` | 1.0.0 | Data Model; scheduling contract; import normalization; Settings; Objective UI; overlap algorithm | Assigned LLD detail | Beta retains canonical UTC persistence but removes the mandatory half-hour boundary because imported and manually reviewed source times must not be silently changed. Time-zone representation, configuration, daylight-saving behavior, and input controls belong in the LLD. |
| `ALPHA:DATA-008` | Merged `main` | Required text PK/FK/enumerated/JSON facts are validated at the DB boundary. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0018` | 1.0.0 | Data Model; persistence LLD; service transaction contract; import pipeline; validation tests | Assigned LLD detail | Beta retains mandatory persistence invariants but removes the premature text-ID constraint and distinguishes database-enforceable structure from domain-semantic validation performed transactionally before commit. The constraint allocation belongs in the LLD. |
| `ALPHA:DATA-009` | Merged `main` | Referenced history is normally archived/cancelled/superseded/rolled back rather than silently deleted. | Replace | `BETA-REQ-0019` | 1.0.0; general purge policy 1.1.0 | Product Contract; retention contract; audit contract; domain lifecycle specifications; destructive-action UI | Assigned LLD detail | Beta makes preservation the default, narrows hard deletion to eligible unprocessed manual records or explicit domain corrections, and requires reviewed, confirmed, transactional, audited dependent deletion. General purge policy remains deferred to Beta 1.1. |
| `ALPHA:DATA-010` | Merged `main` | Mutable logistics master data shall not rewrite historical delivery/pickup evidence; operations preserve immutable site snapshots where needed. | Retain | `BETA-REQ-0020` | 1.0.0 | Inventory Data Model; Dispatch Location contract; Spare Request lifecycle; Fault Tag workflow; audit/history views | Assigned LLD detail | The immutable-evidence rule remains valid. In Beta terminology, snapshots capture the effective Dispatch Location, including an inherited Site address where applicable. Snapshot structure, capture timing, and current-versus-historical presentation belong in the LLD. |
